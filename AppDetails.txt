## 📌 Introduction

**DukanFlow** (internally `ShopManagerApp`) is a production-grade, modern point-of-sale (POS), billing, inventory, and ledger management mobile application built with **React Native (Expo SDK 54)** and **TypeScript**. 

Designed specifically for single and multi-branch retail shops, mobile & electronics outlets, wholesale distributors, and departmental stores, DukanFlow empowers business owners to take complete control of their retail operations—**without requiring continuous internet connectivity**. 

It runs on an **offline-first local architecture** using **SQLite (via Drizzle ORM)** for blazing-fast local read/write execution, coupled with real-time multi-device cloud synchronization powered by **Supabase**.

---

## 📖 Description & Problem Solved

Traditional retail and POS solutions are often clunky, heavily dependent on 24/7 internet connections, or fail to provide robust offline capabilities when local connectivity drops. 

**DukanFlow** bridges this gap:
- **Zero Downtime**: Operates 100% offline. Generate bills, register customers, update stock, and record payments anytime, anywhere.
- **Accurate Financial Health**: Unified cash-in-hand, bank balances (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank accounts), and customer khata (credit ledger) tracking in real time.
- **Data Integrity**: Atomic local transactions, conflict-free multi-device synchronization (Last-Write-Wins), and automated local/cloud backup mechanisms.
- **Device-Bound Security**: Cryptographically verified offline license activation, role-based access control (Owner vs. Salesman), and PIN-guarded actions.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🛒 1. Billing & Point of Sale (POS)
- **Fast Search & Barcode Scanning**: Search products by name, category, or scan via built-in camera barcode/QR scanner.
- **Flexible Unit Management**: Supports item quantities in multiple units (`pcs`, `kg`, `meter`, `box`, `litre`, `dozen`, etc.).
- **Dynamic Pricing & Discounts**: Custom sale prices, discounts, and real-time margin/profit calculation during billing.
- **Customer Association**: Walk-in sales or mapped to registered customer accounts with partial or full credit balances.
- **Bill Search & Filtering**: Deep database search by Bill ID (`#123` or `123`), customer name, or payment methods.

### 📦 2. Inventory & Stock Control
- **Product Management**: Track purchase price, retail price, stock levels, custom units, categories, and barcodes.
- **Automated Low-Stock Alerts**: Visual badges and stock indicators alerting when items need reordering.
- **Automated Barcode/QR Generation**: Generates unique system barcodes and QR payloads automatically if absent.
- **Bulk Stock Adjustments & Cost History**: Accurate profit reporting based on historical purchase prices recorded at bill time.

### 👥 3. Khata & Customer Credit Ledger
- **Customer Directory**: Deep database search across names, phone numbers, CNIC, and addresses.
- **Customer Statement Reports**: Full editorial customer statements (PDF/Print) matching professional billing invoices.
- **Flexible Ledger Repayments**: Record customer debit/credit transactions with detailed notes (e.g., *"Payment received from Ali"*).
- **Automated Reversal**: Complete ledger reversal and recalculation upon bill cancellations or payment edits.

### 💰 4. Multi-Account Cash Flow & Expenses
- **Account Profiles**: Track multiple physical or digital accounts (Cash in Till, Bank Accounts, JazzCash, EasyPaisa).
- **Transaction History**: Real-time record of all inflows, outflows, ledger settlements, and operational expenses.
- **Expense Categorization**: Categorize daily shop expenditures (rent, utilities, tea, salaries, vendor supplies).
- **Net Balance Calculation**: Automated ledger reconciliation ensuring cash flow always mirrors reality.

### 🖨️ 5. Printing & Digital Sharing
- **Thermal Receipt Printing**: Native 58mm / 80mm ESC/POS thermal printer integration via Bluetooth.
- **Dynamic Shop Headers**: Automatically embeds shop title, owner name, salesman, contact numbers, and shop address.
- **Full Invoice Printing & PDF Generation**: Generates clean, branded PDF invoices with print and native share sheet integration.

### 🏪 6. Multi-Shop Switching
- Effortlessly manage and switch between multiple retail branches or shop profiles within a single tap.
- Dedicated data isolation per shop while keeping unified global owner reporting.

### 🔐 7. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Security
- **Owner Mode**: Full, unrestricted access to analytics, staff setup, license management, and settings.
- **Salesman Mode**: PIN-secured mode restricting access to sensitive financial metrics, profit margins, cost prices, or system settings.
- **12 Granular Permissions**: Custom toggles per staff member (e.g., `create_bill`, `edit_product`, `view_profit`, `manage_expenses`).

## ☁️ Services & Architecture

| Layer | Technology | Role & Functionality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mobile Core** | React Native, Expo SDK 54, React 19 | Cross-platform mobile runtime with high performance and smooth UI animations. |
| **Local Database** | SQLite (`expo-sqlite`), Drizzle ORM | High-speed local database engine running full ACID transactions and queries offline. |
| **Cloud Backend** | Supabase (PostgreSQL) | Real-time cloud sync, authenticated multi-device sync, and secure remote backup. |
| **Security & Cryptography** | `expo-crypto`, `expo-secure-store` | Hardware-backed key store, SHA-256 HMAC offline license signature validation. |
| **Document & Printing** | `expo-print`, `expo-sharing` | Thermal ESC/POS formatting, dynamic HTML-to-PDF rendering and document distribution. |
| **File System Backup** | `expo-file-system`, SAF | Android Storage Access Framework & iOS native file export (`.shopbak` package format). |
